# Context

Kalkberg is an online shop for bouldering and climbing gear, based in Flanders. The webshop went live on 2023-11-01 and ships to Belgium, the Netherlands, France, Germany and Luxembourg. It sells climbing shoes, chalk, crash pads, hardware (harnesses, ropes, quickdraws, belay devices, helmets), clothing, training gear, accessories, books and gift cards, under several brands and its own label Kalkberg Basics.

Marketing uses Google Ads, Meta Ads, influencers (paid per month, each with a discount code), an affiliate network (8% commission), SEO/content and email (Salesforce Marketing Cloud). Web analytics run on GA4. Only visitors who accept analytics cookies are tracked.

Kalkberg's management wants to know what marketing really delivers, and every department has its own questions. These are the questions they asked, grouped by who asks them.

## Management (CEO / CMO)

1. How do revenue, orders, average order value and new customers develop month by month, and how does each year compare with the year before?
2. Are we on track against the monthly revenue and new-customer targets?
3. How much did we spend on marketing in total, and what is our blended return (revenue ÷ marketing cost) per month?
4. What does it cost us to acquire a new customer (CAC), per channel and over time?
5. Which share of revenue comes from new versus returning customers?
6. How much is a customer worth over 12 and 24 months (customer lifetime value), and does that depend on the channel that brought them in?
7. Was Black Friday 2025 better than Black Friday 2024 once you take discounts and returns into account?
8. If we had €10.000 extra marketing budget next month, where should it go?

## Performance marketing

1. What are cost, clicks, conversions, CPC, cost per order and ROAS per platform, campaign and month?
2. Google Ads and Meta each report their own conversions. How do those compare with the orders GA4 and the webshop see?
3. How different is the picture under first-click, last-click, linear and position-based attribution? Which channels win or lose depending on the model?
4. How long and how varied are customer journeys before a first order versus a repeat order?
5. Did replacing Google Shopping with Performance Max (February 2025) improve results?
6. Did the new Meta agency (from February 2025) do better than the previous one?
7. How much traffic, revenue and how many new customers does each influencer bring in, and does their monthly fee pay off? Compare tracked links with discount-code use.
8. Are affiliate customers profitable once you count the 8% commission and their discount codes?
9. Do paid channels bring customers who come back, or mostly one-time bargain hunters?
10. How do conversion rates differ by device and country?

## Email and CRM (Salesforce Marketing Cloud)

1. How many subscribers do we have, how does the list grow, and how many unsubscribe or bounce each month?
2. What are the delivery, open, click and unsubscribe rates per newsletter, campaign and journey, and how do they trend?
3. How much revenue can we link to email, per send and per journey (welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase, win-back)?
4. Did each journey make a difference after it went live? Compare before and after its start date.
5. Since 2025-03-01 we only send newsletters to engaged subscribers. Did that improve engagement and revenue per send, and how many people did we stop reaching?
6. Can we trust our open rates? What part of the opens comes from real people?
7. How many subscribers are also customers, and do subscribers buy more often than non-subscribers?
8. At what time of day and on which weekday do people open and click?
9. Which language versions of our emails perform best?

## Customers and products (e-commerce manager)

1. How many unique customers do we really have, and how many buy a second time? How long does it take before they do?
2. How well do monthly customer cohorts keep buying (retention by month since first order)?
3. Which RFM segments (recency, frequency, monetary value) do we have, and how many customers move between segments over time?
4. Which products and categories sell best, and which products often appear in the same order?
5. What is the return rate per category, brand, product and size? Why are climbing shoes returned, and how often do customers order two sizes and send one back?
6. Which products sell well in revenue but badly in margin?
7. How do the January sale, summer sale and spring promotion affect volume, margin and returns?
8. Did the new free-shipping threshold (from 2025-01-15) change the average order value or the number of small orders?
9. How many orders are placed without an account, and are those really new customers?
10. Where do our customers live (country, region, city), and does that differ per channel?

## Finance (CFO)

1. How does actual marketing spend compare with the budget per channel and month, including the revised Q4 2025 Meta budget?
2. What is the gross margin per month, category and channel after discounts, refunds and shipping?
3. How much revenue do we give away in discounts, per discount type (automatic promotions, welcome code, influencer codes, affiliate code, customer-service code)?
4. How much money is refunded per month, and how long does it take from order to refund?
5. How much did the yearly price increases (every 15 January) contribute to revenue growth, compared with volume growth?
6. Gift cards are sold but are not revenue until they are used. How much do we sell per month?
7. Which numbers in the marketing reports should finance not trust, and why?

## Data and trust

1. Do the webshop, GA4, Google Ads, Meta and Marketing Cloud agree on orders and revenue? Where do they differ, and why?
2. What share of orders can we link to a website visit, and to a known customer?
3. Did the platform migration on 2024-09-02 change anything in the data that affects trends over time?
4. Can we link an email click to the website visit and the order that followed?
5. How should a marketer tag campaigns from now on so these reports keep working?
6. Which numbers should come with a warning label on the dashboard?
